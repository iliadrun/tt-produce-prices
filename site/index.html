Prices and volumes are collected and published by NAMIS, a service of NAMDEVCO (the National Agricultural Marketing and Development Corporation of Trinidad and Tobago). This is an independent, free, non-commercial presentation of that public data — always confirm prices at the market.

Prices are wholesale (what vendors pay at Macoya), in Trinidad and Tobago dollars. Retail prices at markets and groceries will be higher. The “in shops and markets” lines inside each item come from NAMDEVCO’s monthly retail survey of farmers’ markets, municipal markets, vege-marts and supermarkets — best-grade produce where graded, so your shop may charge less for lower grades.
